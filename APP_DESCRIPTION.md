# SourceView Together

A Bible you read by voice. Every word is attributed to who spoke it — 774 voices across 365 stories, in four source colors.

---

## What this app is

SourceView Together presents Scripture as a living conversation: narrator, divine speech, named principals, and everyone else, each in its own source color. Read a story in about fifteen to twenty minutes. Read alone, or sit together and take a color.

---

## Read by voice

Unlike a wall of verses, the text is laid out so you can see who is speaking:

- **Narrator** — setting the scene
- **Divine speech** — God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit
- **Principals** — the named people at the center of the story
- **Everyone else** — supporting voices

Character names sit with their words. The story reads like a conversation, because that is what it is.

---

## 365 stories on a thread

The Bible is organized as **365 narrative stories**, not chapter checkboxes. Each story is a complete arc — Creation, not “Genesis 1 then Genesis 2.” Follow the year on a visual thread. Open a division, pick a story, and keep going.

---

## Cast

Meet the voices of Scripture as you read. Cast shows who you have heard, how often they speak, and where they appear. Attribution is the product: 774 distinct sources, not a skin on a study Bible.

---

## Read together

Sit with family, a class, or a small group. Open the same story. Each person takes a source color and reads that voice aloud. The colors do the coordination. After the story, **Talk about it** offers discussion questions for family, school, or small group.

---

## Plans, streak, and reactions

- Flexible **reading plans** and shorter challenges for a season
- A **daily streak** that follows your local calendar
- Optional **daily reminders** on this device
- **Emoji reactions** and notes attached to the words that moved you
- English and French

Progress, notes, and reactions stay on your device.

---

## Use it this way

**On your own.** Open today’s story, read for fifteen minutes, react as you go, and come back tomorrow.

**With others.** Pass the phone or each person opens the story. Assign source colors. Read the scene out loud. Use Talk about it when you want a prompt.

**With a plan.** Start a year, a season, or a short challenge. Pause when life is loud. Resume without guilt.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it free?**  
Yes. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

**Which translation?**  
New Living Translation (NLT), chosen for clarity in a spoken, attributed format.

**Does it work offline?**  
Yes. Reading, plans, notes, and reactions work without a network. Network is used for optional Bible downloads (for example French) and app updates.

**How is my data stored?**  
On your device in a local database. Reading progress, reactions, and preferences are not uploaded to a SourceView account.

**What ages?**  
Built for teens and adults. Families often read together, with a grown-up taking a source color alongside younger readers.

**Is it the whole Bible?**  
The 365 stories cover the biblical narrative from Genesis to Revelation — major events, teachings, and themes in complete story arcs.
