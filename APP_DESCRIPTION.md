## The Problem with Traditional Bible Apps

Most Bible apps approach Scripture like a textbook:
- **Chapter-and-verse format** that fragments the narrative
- **No context** for who's speaking or when
- **Isolated reading** that misses the relational aspect of studying together
- **Checkbox mentality** that prioritizes completion over comprehension
- **No memory of what impacted you** or where God spoke

**SourceView solves all of these problems.**

## What Makes SourceView Different?

### 🎭 **1. Script-Style Reading: See Who's Speaking**

Unlike traditional Bible apps that present Scripture as one continuous block of text, SourceView formats the Bible like a movie script:

- **Color-coded speakers** make it instantly clear who's talking:
  - **Black**: Narrator (setting the scene)
  - **Red**: God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit speaking
  - **Green**: Main character or protagonist
  - **Blue**: Supporting characters and other speakers

- **Speech bubble format** with visual tails showing who said what
- **Character names** appear above their words
- **Natural reading flow** that brings the narrative to life

**Why this matters**: Your congregation will finally understand the flow of conversation in passages like John 3, clearly distinguishing between Jesus' words, Nicodemus's questions, and John's commentary—something that's confusing in traditional formats.

---

### 📖 **2. 365 Story-Based Segments (Not Chapters)**

Most Bible apps divide Scripture by chapters—an artificial structure added 800 years after the Bible was written. SourceView organizes the Bible into **365 natural narrative segments**:

- **15-20 minutes each**: Perfect for daily reading that fits real life
- **Complete story arcs**: Each segment tells a full narrative, not arbitrary chapter breaks
- **365 stories = 1 year**: Read through Scripture's key narratives in one year
- **Context preserved**: Stories flow naturally without artificial interruptions

**Example**: Instead of reading "Genesis 1" and "Genesis 2" as separate chapters, you read "Creation" as one complete story, then "The First Sin" as the next complete narrative.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Is SourceView free?**
Yes! SourceView is completely free for individuals, families, and churches. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

### **What Bible translation does it use?**
SourceView uses the New Living Translation (NLT), chosen for its clarity and readability while maintaining theological accuracy. It's particularly effective for the script-style format and engaging younger readers.

### **Can I use it offline?**
Yes! Download your reading plan, and read even without internet. Group reading requires connection only for real-time sync.

### **Does SourceView replace my physical Bible?**
SourceView is a complement, not a replacement. Many users find it easier for daily reading and engagement, while still using physical Bibles for in-depth study or church services.

### **How is my data stored?**
All notes, reactions, and progress are stored locally on your device. Only group reading sessions transmit data (and only participation info, not personal notes). You can optionally create an account to sync across devices.

### **What ages is it appropriate for?**
SourceView is designed for ages 12+, though younger children enjoy it in family group reading with parent guidance. The emoji system and visual format particularly resonate with teens and young adults.

### **Can I add more Bible translations?**
Additional translations are on our roadmap. We're starting with NLT to perfect the experience, then will add others based on user requests.

### **Is it really 365 stories for the whole Bible?**
The 365 stories cover the complete biblical narrative from Genesis to Revelation, including all major events, teachings, and themes. Some shorter epistles might be combined into one story, while longer narratives like Exodus are divided into multiple complete stories.

### **How does group reading work remotely?**
Group reading works seamlessly for both in-person and remote participants. As long as everyone has the app and the session PIN, they can join from anywhere. The host controls when to advance, and everyone's screen updates in real-time.

### **Can multiple people host sessions simultaneously?**
Absolutely! Every user can host unlimited sessions. A church could have 10 different small groups all using group reading mode at the same time with different PINs.
