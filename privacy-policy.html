Privacy Policy for SourceView Together

Effective Date: August 2026
Last Updated: August 2026

Overview

SourceView Together is a Bible reading application designed to enhance your scripture study experience. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. This includes:

  • No personal names or contact information
  • No email addresses or phone numbers
  • No user accounts or login credentials
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics sent to external services
  • No device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • No data that could identify you personally

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

SourceView Together stores the following information only on your device using a local SQLite database:

Reading Progress Data

  • Bible segments you have completed
  • Reading plans and challenges you've started
  • Your reading streaks and achievements
  • Emoji reactions you've added to Bible passages
  • Reading session history and statistics
  • Book completion status

App Preferences

  • Your selected Bible version (default: NLT)
  • App language settings (English or French)
  • Current reading position
  • Display preferences (font size, appearance, orientation)

Important: All this data remains on your device and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

Permissions

SourceView Together does not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts permissions. The app requires only network access for downloading optional Bible translations and receiving app updates.

Data Security

  • All user data is stored using SQLite with industry-standard security practices
  • Data is protected by your device's built-in security features
  • No data is transmitted over the internet except for Bible content downloads and app updates

Children's Privacy

SourceView Together is designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children under 13:

  • We do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children
  • All data remains on the user's device
  • No online interactions with strangers are possible within the app

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Resetting the app: Clear all reading progress through app settings
  • Uninstalling the app: Removes all data from your device
  • Device reset: Standard device factory reset removes all app data
