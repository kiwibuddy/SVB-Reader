# Privacy Policy for SourceView Together

**Effective Date:** December 2024  
**Last Updated:** December 2024

## Overview

SourceView Together ("we," "our," or "the app") is a Bible reading application designed to enhance your scripture study experience through individual and group reading features. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.** This includes:
- ❌ Personal names or contact information
- ❌ Email addresses or phone numbers
- ❌ User accounts or login credentials
- ❌ Location data beyond device permissions
- ❌ Usage analytics to external services
- ❌ Device identifiers for tracking purposes
- ❌ Any data that could identify you personally

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

SourceView Together stores the following information **only on your device** using local SQLite database:

### Reading Progress Data
- Bible segments you have completed
- Reading plans and challenges you've started
- Your reading streaks and achievements
- Emoji reactions you've added to Bible passages
- Reading session history and statistics
- Book completion status

### App Preferences
- Your selected Bible version (default: NLT)
- App language settings
- Current reading position
- Display preferences

### Group Reading Data (When Participating)
- Temporary session data for group reading experiences
- Your assigned reader role during group sessions
- QR code session identifiers (deleted after session ends)

**Important:** All this data remains on your device and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

## Permissions We Request

### Camera Permission
- **Purpose:** To scan QR codes for joining group Bible reading sessions
- **When Used:** Only when you actively choose to join a group reading session
- **Data Storage:** QR code data is processed temporarily and discarded after joining the session
- **Control:** You can deny this permission and still use all individual reading features

### Future Bluetooth Permission (Planned Feature)
- **Purpose:** To enable device-to-device communication for group reading sessions
- **When Used:** Only when you choose to host or join Bluetooth-based group sessions
- **Data Sharing:** Only reading session coordination data, no personal information
- **Control:** This feature will be entirely optional

## Data Security

### Local Data Protection
- All user data is stored using SQLite with industry-standard security practices
- Data is protected by your device's built-in security features
- No data is transmitted over the internet except for:
  - App updates through official app stores
  - Bible content downloads (if needed)

### No External Data Transmission
- We do not operate servers that collect user data
- We do not use analytics services that track user behavior
- We do not integrate with social media platforms for data collection
- We do not use advertising networks that collect personal information

## Third-Party Services

### Expo Platform
SourceView Together is built using Expo, which may collect anonymous technical data for app functionality:
- Crash reports (containing no personal information)
- Performance metrics (device type, OS version)
- App update delivery

### Apple App Store / Google Play Store
- App downloads and updates are handled by Apple/Google
- These platforms have their own privacy policies
- We do not receive personal information from these platforms

## Children's Privacy

SourceView Together is designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children under 13:
- We do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children
- All data remains on the user's device
- No online interactions with strangers are possible within the app
- Parents can review all data by checking the app's local storage through device settings

## Group Reading Privacy

### QR Code Sessions
- QR codes contain only session identifiers, not personal information
- Session data is temporary and automatically deleted after the reading session ends
- Participants see only reading roles (e.g., "Narrator," "Character 1"), not personal identities

### Future Bluetooth Sessions (Planned)
- Device connections will use temporary, anonymous identifiers
- Only reading coordination data will be shared between devices
- No personal information will be transmitted
- Sessions will be automatically cleaned up after completion

## Data Retention and Deletion

### Automatic Data Management
- Reading session data is automatically cleaned up after completion
- Temporary files are regularly deleted
- Only persistent reading progress data remains (which you control)

### Manual Data Deletion
You can delete your data at any time by:
1. **Resetting the app:** Clear all reading progress through app settings
2. **Uninstalling the app:** Removes all data from your device
3. **Device reset:** Standard device factory reset removes all app data
