SourceView Together colours the whole Bible by who is speaking. Hand four students a colour each and a chapter of Acts becomes something the room performs — with no preparation, no accounts and no cost.

Four roles, four colours

Narrator
Carries the story between the speeches
God
Divine speech, including Jesus
Named people
Moses, Ruth, Peter, Paul — anyone named
Everyone else
Crowds, soldiers, unnamed voices

A student never has to work out whether it is their turn. Their colour is on the screen in front of them, so reading aloud carries no social risk.

What happens in the room

Open the story
Everyone opens the same story on their own device. Nothing to join, no code to read out.
Take a colour
Four students take a colour each. Their turns light up; everything else dims. Rotate the colours next week.
Talk about it
Four questions written for a school room appear at the end. No preparation, and nothing to print.

Before you ask IT or your safeguarding lead

Free. No accounts, no sign-in and no personal data leaves the device — there is nothing to administer and no user records to hold. The English Bible ships inside the app, so a lesson does not depend on the school network.
