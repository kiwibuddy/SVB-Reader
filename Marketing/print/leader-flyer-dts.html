The Bible in 365 complete stories, coloured by who is speaking. Two of the plans happen to be the length of the two halves of a DTS — an Old Testament overview that runs the lecture phase, and a New Testament journey that runs outreach.

Old Testament Journey

Genesis through Habakkuk across 22 books — creation, exodus, the judges, the kings, the psalms and the prophets. No story in it runs longer than 13 minutes.
Outreach · 8 weeks
New Testament Journey
40stories

Luke through Revelation across 10 books, each opening with a short introduction — the gospel, Acts, the letters, and the city that is coming.

Ninety-seven stories over nineteen weeks — about fifteen hours of reading across a whole school. Neither plan assumes the other, so a school can run one, both, or start again with a second outreach.

Why it holds up on outreach

The field has no signal
The English Bible ships inside the app. Once it is installed, nothing needs data, wifi or a SIM that works in that country.
Nothing to set up as a team
No accounts, no sign-in, no codes to read out. A team of twelve opens the same story without anyone administering anything.
Nobody is fundraising for it
Free, with nothing to buy inside it. Students who have raised support for six months are not asked for anything more.

Small groups read it out loud, in parts

Every line is coloured by who said it, so a story can be handed out around the room — one person on narration, one on divine speech, and the rest on the named and unnamed people. Turns light up and everything else dims, so a student on a team where English is a second language always knows when their part is coming.
