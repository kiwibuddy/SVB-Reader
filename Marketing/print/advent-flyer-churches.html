Churches, families & small groups

Four voices. Ten minutes.
One story a day to Christmas Eve.

From Sunday 29 November, the free SourceView Together app reads through Advent in 26 stories, from the first promise in Genesis to the manger in Luke. Every line is coloured by who is speaking and every story is split into four reading parts, so four people can read it out loud with nothing to prepare.

Four parts, four colours

Narrator
Carries the story between the speeches
God
Divine speech, including Jesus
Principals
Abraham, Ruth, David, Isaiah — whoever the story centres on
Everyone else
Angels, shepherds, crowds, supporting voices

When a story has fewer kinds of voice, the app splits the busiest one between readers, so all four still have a part, and nobody has to work out whose turn it is.

Two rooms where four people are already together

Families
Around the table
One story after dinner, each person reading a part: a child voices the angels while a grandparent narrates. Four questions written for families follow every story.
Small groups
In fours, out loud
Read the daily story through the week. When you meet, split into fours and read Sunday’s story in parts, then use the four questions written for small groups.

For your notice sheet or newsletter

This Advent we are reading the story together. From Sunday 29 November the free SourceView Together app has one story a day to Christmas Eve: 26 readings of about ten minutes, each with parts for four voices and questions to talk over. Gather your family or three friends, take a colour each and read it out loud. Search “SourceView Together” on the App Store or Google Play.

Good to know

  • Free. No accounts, no sign-in, no personal data leaves the device.
  • The Bible text is inside the app, so it works without Wi-Fi.
  • Late starters choose today’s reading or Day 1.
  • After Christmas Eve, “12 Days of Christmas” runs 25 December to 5 January.
