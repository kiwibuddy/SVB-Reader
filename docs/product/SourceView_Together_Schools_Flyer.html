Read the Bible.Aloud.Together.

A free Bible app designed to help students read complete Bible stories aloud in groups.

One story. Four colours. Every student has a part.

SourceView Together gives each voice in Scripture a different colour, making it easy for students to read a Bible story aloud together.

Four students take a colour. The app shows who speaks, so the group can simply follow along and read.

How it works
NarratorCarries the story between the speeches
GodDivine speech, including Jesus
Named peopleMoses, Ruth, Peter, Paul
Everyone elseCrowds, soldiers, unnamed voices

No word is added, moved or removed.
