This is good news worth celebrating. Bible apps, printed Bibles, podcasts and reading plans are putting Scripture within easy reach of young people. Schools have a beautiful part to play, because they are where habits form: every day, alongside friends. When Scripture becomes part of the rhythm of a school day, a fresh curiosity has room to grow into a lifelong love of God’s Word.

Three calls for every school
1

Read more.

From verses to the whole story.

Every verse belongs to a bigger story. Reading whole passages helps students see the sweep of Scripture, from creation to new creation, and find their place in it.

“They searched the Scriptures day after day.”ACTS 17:11 NLT
2

Read aloud.

Let the Word be heard.

Much of Scripture was first heard in a gathered room. Reading aloud engages the ear as well as the eye, and gives every student a voice.

“God blesses the one who reads the words of this prophecy to the church, and he blesses all who listen.”REVELATION 1:3 NLT
3

Read together.

Make Scripture a reason to gather.

At the Water Gate, Ezra read the Law aloud and the Levites helped the crowd understand. Reading together turns Scripture into shared conversation, and every voice adds something.

“… clearly explained the meaning of what was being read, helping the people understand each passage.”NEHEMIAH 8:8 NLT
A finish line for one school year

Picture the last day of the school year. Every student has heard the whole story of Scripture read aloud, in their own voice and their friends’ voices, and has talked about it with people who care. Not some students. Every student. Adopt one school, start one reading circle, and let it multiply.

Designed for reading
aloud, together

One story. Four colours.
Every student has a part.

In 1899 a publisher printed the words of Jesus in red. SourceView Together, a free Bible app, gives every voice in Scripture its own colour, so a class can read a story aloud in parts. No word is added, moved or removed.

How it serves the three calls
1

Read more

365 complete stories, Genesis to Revelation. The median story takes 10 minutes, so a whole story fits inside form time. A 180-story plan walks a class through the arc of Scripture in one school year.

2

Read aloud

Every line is coloured by who said it. Your turns light up and the rest dims, so reading aloud feels like a script, not a test.

3

Read together

Four students take a colour each and the colours do the coordination. Every story ends with four questions written for a school room, plus family and small-group sets.

Before you ask a principal or safeguarding lead

Free, with no ads and nothing to buy. No accounts, no sign-in, and no personal data leaves the device. The English Bible (NLT) ships inside the app, so a lesson never depends on the school network.

Part of a bigger picture. Use it alongside the Bible apps, reading plans, printed Bibles, Scripture memory and Bible clubs already serving your schools. What it adds is simple and joyful: a way for a whole room to read Scripture aloud, together.
