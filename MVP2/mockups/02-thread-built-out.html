Your data question: yes — who they spoke to, and their longest exchange

Both are computable, and neither is stored — they have to be derived from the order of turns inside each story, which nothing in the app does yet. The trick is excluding narration: The Narrator sits between almost every speech, so raw adjacency just returns him 1,301 times for Jesus. Skip the narration blocks and the real conversational graph appears.
